[ Memory Space: Chinatown AR ]

Website/WebAR LinkLab WebsiteGrand Prize Article

A geolocated WebAR experience on Niantic's 8th Wall as part of a temporary exhibition in LA's Union Station to showcase the history of old Chinatown.

A closer look (and a dev view) of two of the experiences: Peacock Alley and Sang Yuen Store.

  • Webapp in HTML, CSS, JavaScript (on Niantic's 8th Wall)
  • WebAR experiences with A-Frame, 8th Wall modules, and geolocated VPS waypoints
  • Placed volumetric videos of actors and custom 2D/3D assets on the screen, relative to the positioning of scanned waypoints around LA's Union Station
  • Converse with a peacock, powered by an LLM through InWorld's API
  • Implemented event triggering for user events and asset animations

  • [ Javascript, HTML, CSS, A-Frame, Niantic 8th Wall, Blender, Maya ]

with Professor Scott Fisher @ USC's Mobile and Environmental Media Lab (MEML) and friends :)

In collaboration with [Metro Art, The Huntington-USC Institute on California and the West, the Chinese Historical Society of Southern California, and the Huntington Library].
